Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

Your privacy is highly valued. This policy outlines how your data is handled across this platform.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data that you directly provide to us. Specifically, when you sign up via our footer to receive article alerts, we store your email address securely.

2. Local Infrastructure Storage Framework

To maintain an ultra-fast environment without remote database tracking profiles, we utilize minor browser LocalStorage keys. This handles storing personal interface state features (dark mode preferences) and marking whether you have read a story locally on your own machine.

3. Global Metrics Cloud Framework

Live total views and story likes are stored securely inside an encrypted cloud infrastructure provider (Supabase). This count is completely aggregated, anonymized, and contains zero tracking cookies or personal indicators.
